A message for my big brother

Zeni,

I don't know if a website, a message, or a thousand words could fully explain what you mean to me. But before you leave KingShot, I wanted to make something that says what a simple “goodbye” never could.

To me, you were never just another name on a player list. You became like a big brother. And that means something beyond power, rankings, rallies, or any kingdom we helped build.

Maybe one day the notifications will stop, the battles will become memories, and this chapter will be behind us. But I hope you remember that somewhere in this game, you made a difference to someone — to me.

Thank you for being part of my journey, brother. I won't pretend that things will feel exactly the same without you. But I'm glad our paths crossed, and I'll always be grateful that they did.

You don't owe the game forever. You've earned the right to choose your next chapter, to rest, to grow, and to chase whatever makes you happy. Wherever life takes you, I hope it brings you peace, success, and people who value you as much as you deserve.

This isn't the end of our brotherhood. It's just the end of one chapter.

With respect, always — SAE
